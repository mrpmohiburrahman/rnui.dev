## Notes

- **The three re-records are done.** `cherry-blossom-qrcode`, `sphere-waves` and
  `scrollable-shapes` were captured again and deployed on 2026-10-04, each on a new `_v2` Asset path
  and each back on its **original** `id`, so views, votes and anyone's Remembered set survived. Their
  `↻` markers are gone.
- **Four left, and only four.** `linear-sensors`, `shared-transition` and `text-image-morph` have no
  Recording under any caption; `liquid-glass-playground` was the fourth and was added on 2026-10-04.
  `mnist` is the odd one: it **is** on the site, as "Neural Network", but its `source` points at his
  separate `mnist-expo` repo rather than `src/animations/mnist`, so the matching rule below cannot
  tick it. It is re-recorded and live — the row is unticked only because the rule reads the `source`
  URL. Worth a decision rather than a silent exception.
- **Two of the four cannot be recorded on the simulator, and one is not a demo.**
  `linear-sensors` reads `useAnimatedSensor(SensorType.ROTATION)` and `GRAVITY` with no `Platform`
  guard and no `isAvailable` fallback, so the Simulator's absent CoreMotion data leaves it on one
  frozen pose — it needs a physical iPhone. `text-image-morph` is not one of the app's 124 registry
  keys at all: it is a shared component whose only consumer is `the-little-prince`, so the deep link
  answers `Animation "text-image-morph" not found` and its behaviour is already published as The
  Little Prince. Only `shared-transition` is both missing and recordable here.
- **Counting.** The tick is decided by the Recording's `source` URL matching its directory, so a
  caption change never moves a box. 121 of the 125 match, on an exact last-segment match rather than
  a substring one — a substring test ticks `shared-transition` off `instagram-shared-transition` and
  `qrcode` off `empty-qrcode`, both wrong.
- **Two Recordings still carry a `source` slug that never existed** in Enzo's repo, so those links
  404 on the live site. They are listed below; they are why the raw count of Enzo-sourced Recordings
  is higher than 121.
- **Not every animation is a candidate.** Some need a gesture to show anything (`shared-transition`,
  `drag-to-sort`), which makes for a dull Demo. The `add-recording` step-1 gate and your judgement
  decide; the list does not pre-judge them.
