## Notes

- **↻ Three are ticked but going back in.** `sphere-waves`, `scrollable-shapes` and
  `cherry-blossom-qrcode` are live right now and are being removed to be re-recorded. Untick them
  when the rows come out of `data/misc.ts`; they go back in on the **same** `id` (see **Re-record**
  in `CONTEXT.md`), so the counts and anyone's bookmarks survive the round trip.
- **Counting.** The tick is decided by the Recording's `source` URL matching its directory, so a
  caption change never moves a box. 109 of the 125 match. A separate 4
  Recordings carry a `source` slug that **never existed** in Enzo's repo — those links 404 today and
  are listed below; they are why the raw count of Enzo-sourced Recordings is higher than 108.
- **Not every animation is a candidate.** Some need a gesture to show anything (`shared-transition`,
  `drag-to-sort`), which makes for a dull Demo. The `add-recording` step-1 gate and your judgement
  decide; the list does not pre-judge them.
