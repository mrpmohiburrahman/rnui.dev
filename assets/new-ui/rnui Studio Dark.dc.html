09 · THE DIRECTION IN SIX SENTENCES

This is a dark room where 277 phone recordings are the only light in it. It optimises for the recordings: near-black canvas, chrome reduced to hairlines and monospaced labels, and a tile that physically glows in the hue of its own capture when it plays. Density comes from portrait tiles at 208px across — five to a 1440px row, ten above the fold — so the shelf reads as a rack of devices rather than a gallery wall. Metadata is ranked, not balanced: title, then category, then contributor, then a hairline view bar, then the controls that actually do something. The light mode is the same room with the lights on — the media plinth stays near-black in both, because a recording on paper stops being a light source. It deliberately sacrifices the friendliness of a bright interface, decorative colour, and any sense that this is a marketing page.
